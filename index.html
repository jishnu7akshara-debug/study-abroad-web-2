Ten months, five stages

A typical September start, if you come to us the November before.

  1. November

    We get to know you

    Two long conversations about your marks, money, interests and the life you want after the degree. Then an honest read on where you'll get in.

  2. December – January

    Shortlist and tests

    Ten to twelve courses with fees, rankings and job outcomes side by side. We book your IELTS, PTE or GRE and share prep material.

  3. January – March

    Applications

    Your statement of purpose goes through at least three drafts. Every application is tracked in a shared sheet you and your parents can see.

  4. April – June

    Offers, money, decision

    We compare offers, chase scholarships, and line up your education loan so the funds are ready before the visa.

  5. July – August

    Visa and departure

    Visa filed, mock interview done, room booked, forex loaded. A last evening with other students going to the same city.
